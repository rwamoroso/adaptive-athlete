Progress doesn’t come from random hard workouts. It comes from **planned stress applied at the right time**. That’s the science behind **GAS — General Adaptation Syndrome** — and it’s the backbone of how this app builds your training.

Your body adapts to stress in three phases.

First is **alarm**. You introduce a challenge your body isn’t fully prepared for yet: a heavier lift, an extra rep, a faster pace, a longer run. Muscles are stressed. The nervous system works harder. This isn’t damage — it’s a signal.

Next is **resistance**. With proper recovery, your body doesn’t just return to baseline — it adapts above it. Muscles grow stronger, tendons become more resilient, aerobic capacity improves, and effort feels more controlled. This is where real progress happens.

The final phase is **exhaustion**, and smart training avoids living there. Too much stress without enough recovery leads to plateaus, fatigue, and injury. More is not better. **Better is better.**

That’s why progressive overload must be intentional. Progress doesn’t always mean adding weight. It can mean more reps, better technique, improved pacing, or doing the same work with less effort. Sometimes the smartest move is holding steady so your body can fully adapt.

The planning session you’re about to go through is where this precision happens.

By capturing your goals, experience, recovery capacity, and available equipment, the app builds a **progressive overload strategy tailored to your biology**. Every session fits into a larger plan designed to apply just enough stress to trigger adaptation — without pushing you into exhaustion.

This isn’t guesswork. It’s structured adaptation.
